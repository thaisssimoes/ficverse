# Fanfic Management System - User Guide

## Introduction

Welcome to the Fanfic Management System! This platform allows you to create, manage, and publish fanfiction stories with advanced features like rich text editing, draft mode, content warnings, and a comprehensive tagging system.

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Navigate to the registration page
2. Provide a username, email, and password
3. Click "Register" to create your account
4. You'll be automatically logged in

### Logging In

1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Login"

## Creating a Fanfic

### Basic Information

1. Click "Create New Fanfic" from your dashboard
2. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Title**: The name of your fanfic
   - **Synopsis**: A brief description (supports rich text formatting)
   - **Category**: Choose from Romance, Aventura, Drama, Comédia, Ficção Científica, Fantasia, Terror, or Mistério
   - **Disclaimer** (optional): Any disclaimers about your work

### Rich Text Editing

The synopsis, disclaimer, and chapter content fields support rich text formatting:
- **Bold**, *Italic*, Underline, Strikethrough
- Headings (H1, H2, H3)
- Ordered and unordered lists
- Paragraph breaks

### Cover Image

1. Click "Upload Cover" or drag and drop an image
2. Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP
3. Maximum size: 5MB
4. The image will be displayed on your fanfic card

### Tags

Add up to 5 tags in each category:
- **Fandom Tags**: The universe/series your fanfic is based on
- **Warning Tags**: Content warnings (violence, mature themes, etc.)
- **Pairing Tags**: Character relationships featured in your story

To add tags:
1. Start typing in the tag input field
2. Select from existing tags or create a new one
3. Click the tag to add it
4. Remove tags by clicking the X icon

### Content Warnings

If your fanfic contains adult content or sensitive material:
1. Check the "Adult Content" checkbox
2. Add specific trigger warnings in the text field
3. Readers will see a warning modal before accessing your content

### Draft Mode

By default, new fanfics are created as drafts:
- Drafts are only visible to you
- You can edit and refine your work
- Drafts don't appear in public listings or search results

## Publishing Your Fanfic

### Requirements

Before publishing, ensure you have:
- A title
- A synopsis
- Selected a category

### Publishing Process

1. Click the "Publish" button on your fanfic
2. The system will validate your fanfic
3. Once published:
   - Your fanfic becomes visible to all users
   - It appears in public listings and search results
   - A publication timestamp is recorded

### Unpublishing

You can return a published fanfic to draft mode:
1. Go to your dashboard
2. Find the published fanfic
3. Click "Unpublish"
4. The fanfic will be hidden from public view

## Managing Chapters

### Creating Chapters

1. Open your fanfic
2. Click "Add Chapter"
3. Enter the chapter title and content
4. Choose whether to save as draft or publish immediately

### Draft Chapters

- Draft chapters are only visible to you
- Readers won't see draft chapters in the chapter list
- Publish chapters when they're ready

### Editing Chapters

1. Navigate to the chapter
2. Click "Edit"
3. Make your changes
4. Save or publish

### Deleting Chapters

1. Click "Delete" on the chapter
2. Confirm the deletion
3. Remaining chapters will be automatically reordered

## Editing Your Fanfic

### Updating Metadata

1. Go to your dashboard
2. Click "Edit" on the fanfic
3. Modify any fields:
   - Title, synopsis, disclaimer
   - Category
   - Tags
   - Content warnings
4. Save your changes

### Replacing the Cover

1. In edit mode, click "Change Cover"
2. Upload a new image
3. The old cover will be automatically removed

## Deleting a Fanfic

⚠️ **Warning**: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone!

When you delete a fanfic:
- All chapters are deleted
- All comments are removed
- All questions and answers are deleted
- The cover image is removed
- Tags are unlinked

To delete:
1. Go to your dashboard
2. Click "Delete" on the fanfic
3. Confirm the deletion in the modal

## Discovering Fanfics

### Browsing by Category

1. Use the category filter on the homepage
2. Select a category to see all fanfics in that genre
3. Categories include: Romance, Aventura, Drama, Comédia, Ficção Científica, Fantasia, Terror, Mistério

### Searching by Tags

1. Click on any tag in a fanfic card or detail page
2. View all fanfics with that tag
3. Add multiple tags to narrow your search
4. The system uses AND logic (fanfics must have all selected tags)

### Content Warnings

When viewing a fanfic with content warnings:
1. A warning modal will appear
2. Read the warnings carefully
3. Click "I Understand, Continue" to proceed
4. Click "Go Back" to return to the previous page

## Dashboard Features

Your dashboard shows:
- All your fanfics (published and drafts)
- Draft status badges
- Quick actions:
  - Edit
  - Delete
  - Publish/Unpublish
  - View

### Filtering

- View all fanfics
- Filter by published only
- Filter by drafts only

## Interactive Mode (Advanced)

For interactive fanfics with reader choices:
1. Enable "Interactive Mode" when creating your fanfic
2. Add questions with placeholders in your chapters
3. Readers answer questions to personalize their reading experience
4. See the Interactive Fanfic documentation for details

## Tips and Best Practices

### Writing

- Use rich text formatting to make your content more engaging
- Write clear, descriptive synopses to attract readers
- Use appropriate content warnings to respect reader preferences

### Tags

- Use specific, relevant tags
- Check existing tags before creating new ones
- Use all three tag types (fandom, warning, pairing) for better discoverability

### Drafts

- Use draft mode to work on your fanfic without pressure
- Publish chapters as you complete them
- Keep unfinished work in draft mode

### Categories

- Choose the most appropriate category for your fanfic
- Categories help readers find stories they're interested in
- You can change the category later if needed

## Troubleshooting

### Can't Publish

- Ensure you have a title and synopsis
- Check that you've selected a category
- Verify all required fields are filled

### Cover Upload Failed

- Check the file format (JPEG, PNG, GIF, WEBP only)
- Ensure the file is under 5MB
- Try a different image

### Tags Not Saving

- Ensure you haven't exceeded the 5-tag limit per type
- Check that you've clicked to add the tag
- Refresh the page and try again
